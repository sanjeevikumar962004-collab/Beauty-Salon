The
Apothecary

We curate our backbar with the same rigorous standard applied to our cutting techniques. Only the highest-grade, ethically sourced, and clinically proven formulations touch your hair.

Sulfate Free Paraben Free Cruelty Free Vegan Options

Botanical Alchemy

Harnessing the power of pure plant extracts and essential oils. Our primary care lines rely on nature's intelligence to restore moisture balance, soothe scalp inflammation, and provide antioxidant protection against environmental stressors.

Bond-Building Technology

Where nature meets advanced science. During all chemical and color services, we utilize patented molecular formulas that work on a cellular level to seek out and cross-link broken disulfide bonds, ensuring the structural integrity of your hair is never compromised.

Microbiome Therapy

A healthy scalp is the absolute foundation of beautiful hair. Our targeted serums utilize prebiotics and balancing botanicals to soothe irritation, gently regulate sebum production, and foster the optimal environment for robust, thick hair growth.

Phyto-Protein Fortification

We replace synthetic silicones with weightless, plant-derived proteins and amino-peptides. These intelligent complexes bind directly to the hair cuticle, smoothing frizz, sealing in vital moisture, and providing intense shine without ever compromising your natural movement.
