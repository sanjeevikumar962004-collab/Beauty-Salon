Client Curiosities

For significant color transformations or corrections, a brief consultation is highly recommended. This ensures our artisans can assess hair integrity, perform patch tests if necessary, and allocate the exact time required for perfection.

We partner exclusively with sustainable, high-performance brands that prioritize scalp health and vibrant longevity. All formulations are sulfate-free and ethically sourced.

Arrive with clean, dry hair if you are receiving an updo or dry cut. For color services, hair washed 24 hours prior is ideal as natural oils help protect the scalp during processing.

We kindly request a minimum of 24 hours' notice for adjustments or cancellations. This courtesy allows us to extend the availability to guests on our waiting list.
